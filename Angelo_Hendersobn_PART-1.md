## **Part 1: Proposal for Website Development** 

### **Objective:**  
Each gperson must submit a formal proposal detailing their website concept, including the intended purpose, audience, and planned features.  

### **Deliverables:**  
- **Website Topic & Purpose:** Clearly define the subject of the website and why it is relevant.  
- **Intended Audience:** Describe the target users and how they will interact with the site.  
- **Planned Pages & Navigation Structure:** Outline the five required pages (`index.html`, `history.html`, `originators.html`, `current.html`, `contact.html`).  
- **Wireframe Mockups:** Provide **hand-drawn or digital sketches** of the homepage and at least one subpage.  
- **Color Scheme & Typography Choices:** Justify why the selected design elements are appropriate.  
- **Features & Technologies:** List any planned **JavaScript elements, animations, media, forms, or interactive elements**.  
- **Roles & Responsibilities:** Define how group members will contribute.  

### **Grading Rubric (20 Points Total):**  
| Criteria | Points | Description |
|----------|--------|-------------|
| Clarity of Proposal | 5 | Clearly defines the website topic, purpose, and audience. |
| Navigation & Pages | 5 | Includes a structured outline of required pages and navigation. |
| Wireframes | 5 | Provides well-organized mockups of at least two pages. |
| Features & Aesthetics | 5 | Thoughtfully considers design elements and planned technologies. |


Kansas City Chiefs: The Patrick Mahomes Era Website Proposal
Website Topic: 
The website will focus on the Kansas City Chiefs, specifically their history and key players during the Patrick Mahomes era. It will provide insights into the background stories of star players, their off-the-field investments, and recruiting journeys.

Purpose:  
The website aims to serve as a hub for NFL fans and sports enthusiasts who want to learn more about the Kansas City Chiefs and their key players' stories, philanthropy, and career paths. It will also provide access to upcoming game schedules and links to the athletes' fundraisers.

Intended Audience:
- NFL fans  
- Kansas City Chiefs supporters  
- Sports historians and analysts  
- High school and college athletes seeking inspiration  
- General sports enthusiasts interested in player development and investments  

Navigation & Pages
The website will have a clear and structured outline of pages for easy navigation:
1. Home Page  
   - Overview of the Kansas City Chiefs in the Patrick Mahomes era  
   - Team history since Mahomes joined  
   - Featured images and videos  

2. Player Profiles (Individual pages for each featured player)  
   - Patrick Mahomes  
   - Kareem Hunt
   - Travis Kelce
   - Nick Bolton
   - Chris Jones

   Each profile will include:  
   - Personal background & high school recruiting journey  
   - Business ventures & off-the-field investments  
3. Philanthropy & Investments
   - List of fundraisers and charities supported by each player  
   - Links to donation pages  

4. Upcoming Games
   - schedule of the Chiefs’ upcoming games  
   - Links to ticket purchases and streaming options  

5. Multimedia Gallery
   - Images, videos, and interactive clips showcasing the players’ personal and professional lives  

The website will feature at least two well-organized mockups, including:  
1. Homepage Layout
   - Main navigation bar at the top  
   - Banner image of the Kansas City Chiefs  
   - Sections for team history, featured players, and upcoming games  

2. Player Profile Page Mockup
   - Sidebar navigation for different sections (Bio, NFL Career, Investments, Philanthropy)  
   - Embedded videos and images  
   - External links to fundraisers and player social media  

Features & Aesthetics
Color Scheme & Design:
  - Chiefs' signature red color for lettering and borders  
  - Quality images and background elements  
  - A modern and visually appealing interface  

Technology & Features:
  - Interactive timeline of the Chiefs’ history in the Mahomes era  
  - External links to fundraiser pages
